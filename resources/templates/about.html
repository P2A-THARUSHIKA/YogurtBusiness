About Rithu Milk Products

Our Story

A Decade of Love, Care & Trust

Rithu Milk Products began its journey over 10 years ago with a simple dream — to create delicious yoghurt with love, care, and dedication.

From the very beginning, Rithu has followed a commitment to making yoghurt without using preservatives to extend its shelf life.

What started as a small beginning has grown into a journey built on passion, hard work, and the trust of our customers.

For over a decade, we have continued this journey with the same dedication, bringing our products to customers who have supported and believed in us.

To us, every product represents more than just taste. It represents the love, care, and trust that have kept Rithu growing through the years.

About Rithu

A Journey Built with Love and Dedication

Rithu Milk Products began its journey around 10 years ago with a simple dream of creating delicious yoghurt with love and dedication.

From our humble beginnings to where we are today, we have continued our journey with hard work, commitment and the trust of our customers.

At Rithu, we prepare our yoghurt without using preservatives to extend its shelf life. Our journey continues with the same passion and dedication that inspired us from the beginning.
